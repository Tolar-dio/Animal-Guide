# BirdLife International

**Where:** https://datazone.birdlife.org/contact-us/request-our-data
**Subject:** Bird range maps for a free, non-commercial species guide

Hello,

I'm building animal-guide (https://github.com/Tolar-dio/animal-guide), a free, open-source guide to every vertebrate species with a map on every species page. It has no ads, sponsorships or revenue of any kind.

I'd like to request the BirdLife bird species distribution maps, and written permission under Section 11 of your terms to publish a simplified derivative on the site:

- Each range would be converted to coarse H3 hexagon cells (about 110 km across) and shown only as that hexagon display. The original polygons would not be published or downloadable.
- Every bird map would carry the credit "Range: BirdLife International and Handbook of the Birds of the World" and link to the species' Data Zone page.
- The purpose is public education about where birds live and how threatened they are.

I'm happy to send a preview before launch and to use whatever attribution wording you prefer.
