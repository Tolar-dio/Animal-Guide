# IUCN Red List

**To:** redlist@iucn.org
**Subject:** Permission request: simplified range derivatives on a free conservation-education site

Hello,

I'm building animal-guide (https://github.com/Tolar-dio/animal-guide), a free, open-source, non-commercial guide to every vertebrate species. It carries no ads, sponsorships or paid features. Its goal is conservation education: every species page shows where the animal lives, its Red List category, and links back to its Red List assessment.

I've read the Terms of Use (v3.1) and understand that Section 4 does not allow redistribution of Red List spatial data, including derivative works, without a waiver. I'm writing to ask for that waiver for a narrow use:

1. Converting Red List range polygons for amphibians, sharks and rays, and fish groups into coarse H3 hexagon cells (about 110 km across, roughly 12,000 km² each). The original polygons would never be published or offered for download; only the coarse hexagon display would appear.
2. Showing those hexagons on each species page with the credit "Range: IUCN Red List of Threatened Species, [year]" and a link to the species' assessment.
3. Caching Red List categories and population trends between official Red List updates, refreshed whenever a new version is published.

I'd be glad to share a preview of the map before anything goes live, and to follow any attribution wording you prefer.

Thank you for your time and for the Red List itself.
