About Animal Guide

An open guide to every animal species, built in the spirit of ARKive. It starts with every mammal and will grow to every vertebrate, then every invertebrate.

ARKive held more than 100,000 photos and videos of 16,000 species when it closed in 2019. Its media was donated under terms that could not move to anyone else, so when the money ran out the whole collection went into storage. Animal Guide is built the opposite way: every page is generated from openly licensed data, the site costs nearly nothing to run, and anyone can copy and rebuild it from the source code.

How it works

A Python pipeline pulls the species list from the Mammal Diversity Database, links each species to Wikidata, iNaturalist and GBIF, chooses the best openly licensed photos, converts expert range maps into hexagons, and renders a static page for every species. Nothing on the site is written by hand except the templates.
